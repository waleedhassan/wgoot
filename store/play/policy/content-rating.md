# Content rating — IARC questionnaire answers

**Where:** Play Console → Policy and programmes → App content → Content ratings

The questionnaire is run by IARC and produces ratings for several regions at
once (ESRB, PEGI, USK, ClassInd, GRAC, IARC generic). Answer honestly — a wrong
rating is a policy violation, and you can always re-take the questionnaire.

## Category

Choose **Utility, Productivity, Communication or Other** — not Game, and not
Social. A prayer timetable is a reference utility.

## Answers

| Question | Answer |

## Religious content

The questionnaire has no "religious content" gate — devotional and reference
apps are unrated on that axis. The one thing to keep clean is that the app does
not present religious content that disparages any group, which it does not.

## Expected outcome

**Everyone / PEGI 3 / USK 0 / ESRB Everyone** in every region.

If IARC returns anything higher, an answer above was mis-clicked. Re-take it.

## After a content change

Re-take the questionnaire if you ever add: a comment or community feature, links
that leave the app to unmoderated content, ads, or in-app purchases. Rating is
tied to the app, not to a single release.
