## title

Wqoot — Prayer Times & Adhan

## short_description

Accurate prayer times, adhan alerts and the Hijri date. Fully offline.

## full_description

Wqoot (وَقُوتُ الصَّلاة) is an Arabic prayer times app. It calculates the five daily prayers, sunrise and the Hijri date entirely on your device — no internet connection, no account, no tracking.

The name comes from Imam Malik ibn Anas, who opened his Muwatta with "Kitab Wuqut as-Salah". "Wuqut" is the plural of "waqt", a time.

■ PRAYER TIMES
• The five prayers plus sunrise, computed astronomically on the device itself.
• More than 180 cities, with an Arabic search that ignores diacritics and normalises hamza spellings.
• Each city is computed in its own time zone, so the times stay correct even when your phone is somewhere else.

■ CALCULATION METHODS
• The method is chosen automatically from the city's country: Umm al-Qura, Egyptian General Authority of Survey, Muslim World League, University of Islamic Sciences Karachi, Dubai, Kuwait, Qatar, Diyanet, Singapore, ISNA and more.
• You can override the method manually at any time.
• Asr madhab: majority or Hanafi.
• High latitude rule for locations far from the equator.

■ FINE TUNING
• Adjust any prayer by −60 to +60 minutes to match your local mosque or national calendar.
• Adjustments apply to both the timetable and the notifications.
• Hijri date offset of ±3 days.

■ ADHAN AND ALERTS
• An adhan notification when each prayer time enters.
• An optional reminder 5 to 30 minutes beforehand.
• Mute any single prayer — get Fajr alerts without Dhuhr, for example.
• Alerts are scheduled on an alarm-grade notification channel so they arrive on time, and are rescheduled automatically after a reboot or an app update.

■ HIJRI CALENDAR
• Umm al-Qura calendar with Arabic month names.
• Hijri and Gregorian dates shown together on the home screen.

■ MONTHLY TIMETABLE
• A full month of prayer times in one table, with today highlighted and Fridays marked.

■ HOME SCREEN WIDGET
• A resizable widget showing the next prayer, the countdown to it, and the full timetable for today.

■ LOOK AND FEEL
• A fully right-to-left Arabic interface set in Amiri and IBM Plex Sans Arabic.
• Light and dark themes that follow the system.
• 12 or 24 hour clock, and Eastern Arabic numerals.

■ PRIVACY
• The app collects no data and sends nothing to any server.
• It never asks for location permission — you pick your city from a list.
• No accounts, no sign-in, no ads, no analytics, no tracking.
• Your settings stay on your device.

■ A NOTE ON ACCURACY
Prayer times are astronomical calculations using established methods, and may differ by a few minutes from your mosque's calendar. Follow your mosque's adhan where they differ, or use the manual adjustment to match it exactly.
