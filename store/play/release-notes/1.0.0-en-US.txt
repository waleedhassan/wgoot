The first release of Wqoot.

• The five daily prayers and sunrise, computed on your device, no internet.
• Over 180 cities, with an Arabic search that ignores diacritics.
• Calculation method picked automatically by country, or set by hand.
• Adhan alerts with an optional early reminder, and per-prayer muting.
• Hijri (Umm al-Qura) date and a full monthly timetable.
• A home screen widget with the next prayer and its countdown.
• Light and dark themes, 12/24 hour clock, fully Arabic interface.
